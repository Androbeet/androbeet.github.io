Who I am

Androbeet (Andrew) is a self-taught writer, philosopher, and content creator. His work is about naming the unnamed — the gaps in our emotional and political vocabulary that leave people without language for their own experience.

What I write about

Philosophy, atheism, feminism, and the psychology of belief — why people inherit the ideas they inherit, and what it costs them to keep them.
